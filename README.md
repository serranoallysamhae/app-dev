## **Overview**
*Stranger Things* is a **sci-fi horror series** created by the Duffer Brothers. It premiered on **Netflix** in **2016** and quickly became a cultural phenomenon.

---

### **Main Features**
- **Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery
- **Setting:** Hawkins, Indiana (1980s)
- **Key Themes:**
  - Friendship
  - Parallel dimensions
  - Government conspiracies

---

#### **Top Characters**
1. **Eleven** – The girl with telekinetic powers  
2. **Mike Wheeler** – Loyal friend and leader  
3. **Jim Hopper** – The town’s chief of police  

---

> *“Friends don’t lie.”* – Eleven

---

### **Why I Love It**
- Nostalgic 80s vibe  
- Great mix of suspense and emotional depth  
- Amazing soundtrack featuring classics like *The Police* and *Kate Bush*
